## Executive Summary
The Thermal Matrix governs the intelligent routing of active power loads up to 18kW directly to the physical water heater elements. It utilizes bare-metal thermal telemetry to predict and prevent thermal runaway before hardware thresholds are breached.

## Security Status
- **Thermal Control Logic:** SECURED IN PRIVATE VAULT.
- **Hardware Integration:** 18kW Solid-State Relay (SSR) modulation.
- **Fail-Safe:** Hardwired directly into the Kinetic Guillotine safety relays.
