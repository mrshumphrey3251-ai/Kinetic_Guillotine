# HVF EXECUTIVE BLUEPRINT: CERBERUS TREASURY MATRIX
**Lead Architect & CEO:** Jeffery Humphrey
**Entity:** Humphrey Virtual Farms LLC

## 1. ZERO-TRUST EDGE SECURITY
The Kinetic Guillotine nodes operate in remote physical locations. To mitigate physical vector attacks, HVF operates on a Zero-Trust Treasury Protocol. Our edge compute nodes hold zero digital assets locally. 

## 2. AUTOMATED COLD SWEEPS
Upon completion of computational workloads (cryptographic hashing or AI rendering), all generated digital assets are instantaneously swept from the edge hardware via the encrypted RF Mesh. 

## 3. MULTI-SIGNATURE COLD STORAGE
Assets are routed directly into the Cerberus Treasury—an air-gapped, multi-signature cold storage vault entirely disconnected from the internet. This ensures that even in the event of a total localized hardware compromise, the corporate treasury remains 100% secure, immutable, and sovereign.
