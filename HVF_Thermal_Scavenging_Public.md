**Lead Architect & CEO:** Jeffery Humphrey

## 1. SOVEREIGN THERMAL EQUILIBRIUM
The HVF Biogas system achieves zero energy waste through Combined Heat and Power (CHP). The biological reactor requires constant heat (35°C - 40°C), while the 1,800 RPM prime mover generates extreme exhaust heat. 

## 2. BARE-METAL ACTUATION
To eliminate human error and grid dependency, the thermal loop is governed by a proprietary C++ bare-metal controller. This software continuously analyzes digester telemetry and autonomously actuates hydronic solenoids to route engine exhaust heat precisely where it is needed.

By mapping physical thermodynamics to compiled C++ logic, the Kinetic Guillotine micro-grid operates at absolute peak efficiency 24/7.
